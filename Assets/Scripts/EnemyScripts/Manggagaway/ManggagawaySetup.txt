MANGGAGAWAY MAIN BOSS

Starter assets:
Tools > Manggagaway > Create Starter Assets
Assets/Prefabs/Enemies/Manggagaway contains the boss, definition, separate HUD bar,
and seven spell visuals (including the beam charge). The command creates missing
assets only; it never overwrites customized assets or edits your open scene.
The visible squares are placeholders, not final boss/spell art.

Scene setup:
1. Place EnemyManggagaway under the intended RoomInstance. Alternatively assign
   Room explicitly; a generated room can also be resolved through RoomManager.
2. Place MainBossHealthBar under the existing screen-space HUD Canvas.
   Leave the existing mini-boss bar unchanged. Track Main Boss is enabled only
   on the new bar. Its health/name appear only in the main boss's room.
3. Assign enemy prefabs to Summon Pool. An empty pool disables summoning and
   produces a startup warning. Set the maximum living summons/count as desired.
4. Set EnemyHealth's base max HP/resistances, EnemyLevelXP's level/reward, and
   EnemyElevationLevel. The starter base max HP is 500, with normal level scaling.
5. The room must have registered AStarManager walkable/detection tilemaps.
   Set Blocking Layers and Clearance Radius for the boss's physical footprint.
   Main bosses are excluded from regular random enemy/adaptation pools.

Boss animation:
Open ManggagawayDefinition. Assign a sheet, cell size and frames per direction
for each state, then use that state's Slice And Assign button.
Grid reading order is left-to-right, top-to-bottom.
Idle, Walk, Attack and Transformation expect consecutive direction groups:
SouthWest, South, SouthEast, East, NorthEast, North, NorthWest, West.
Second Form Idle/Walk/Attack and Dead expect East followed by West only.
Left-facing directions use West, right-facing use East; moving straight
up/down keeps the last horizontal side. Transformation uses the first-form
eight directions.
Each state can have a different number of frames. Definition FPS controls
playback; transformation waits for all frames in the selected direction.
Missing transformation art uses the inspector fallback duration (1.5 seconds).
Idle/walk/attack loop; transformation/dead hold their final frame.
The boss renders through one SpriteRenderer, without Animator or paper dolls.

Spell visuals:
Each spell prefab has SpriteRenderer and ManggagawaySpellVisual. Assign the
Sprites array, Frames Per Second and Loop. Reset renderer tint if your art
should render without the placeholder color. Visual playback does not determine
spell cooldown, projectile lifespan or healing ticks.
The beam also uses the existing Beam component and hostile-player damage path.
Leave Beam's own Beam Sprites empty when using ManggagawaySpellVisual so the
boss's Beam Duration remains authoritative. Beam art faces local +X.
AOE damage is an immediate single radial hit; its visual lasts one second.
Summoning and teleport effects last one second. Heal visual lasts while healing.
Size each visual prefab to match its configured gameplay area/range.

Behavior and defaults:
- First-form HP cannot fall below floor(MaxHealth * 0.45), minimum 1 HP.
  Even a lethal hit starts transformation, not death. Damage is blocked during
  transformation; completion restores full HP and permanently enters form two.
  Only form two can die. Cooldowns persist through transformation.
- Projectile: default attack in range, homing, 2-second life, 1.5-second cooldown.
- Beam: 35% configurable chance instead of a ready projectile, when beam is also
  ready; 1.5-second charge, 8-second cooldown. It aims at the player after charging.
- AOE: priority when the detected player is within AOE Range; 10-second cooldown.
- Summon: random prefab/position, randomized initial delay, 30-second cooldown
  thereafter. Requires a currently detected living player in the same room.
- Heal: after a damaged boss sees the player leave detection vicinity, heal
  10 HP every second for up to 30 seconds; 45-second cooldown from cast start.
  Damage interrupts healing, not just the player returning. Full HP also stops it.
  It must see a player leave again to start a new healing attempt.
- Teleport: 30-second cooldown; 10 successive damaging hits OR damage totaling
  30% of HP at the start of the sequence. A gap over 5 seconds resets the sequence.
  Both thresholds and the gap are configurable. A successful teleport resets it.
  Searches 64 random points across the whole room's bounds for walkable cell
  centers at the same elevation/inside the same room, outside blocking colliders,
  at least 6 units from the player and farther than the current position, then
  picks the farthest. It also fires when the player attacks from outside
  detection range. No safe tile means no teleport; it warns and retries
  after one second rather than using an invalid destination.
- Death: after the Dead frames play, holds for Death Linger Duration, fades out
  over Death Fade Duration, then the boss is destroyed (same pattern as the Lich).
- The boss chases detected players via existing A* paths outside projectile range.
  It never summons before spotting a player, and does not attack across rooms.
  Projectile, beam, and radial damage use the existing elevation-aware hostile
  damage helper. Missing required projectile/beam behavior reports an error.

Verification:
Tools > Manggagaway > Run Checks validates phase protection, refill/final death,
ordinary enemy health compatibility, directional sharing, healing cancellation,
starter spell defaults/prefab wiring and independent HUD binding.
Play-test final art timing, collision clearance, room navigation, attack ranges,
summon balance and HUD placement with your chosen room and sprite assets.
